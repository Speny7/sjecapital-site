Company car tax calculator for directors 2026/27

See the tax on a company car, and whether it leaves you better or worse off than running your own car and claiming mileage. Every step of the working is shown, using HMRC's 2026/27 rates.

A like-for-like comparison. Both options are measured in the same after-tax money: how much less you end up with personally each year. The company’s Corporation Tax relief is already counted, because every pound it spends on the car is a pound it cannot pay you as a dividend.

Where to find it
Where to find itOn the car’s V5C logbook (section V.7), the manufacturer’s specification, or a lease quote. For a car you already have, the DVLA’s free online vehicle checker shows it from the number plate. Use the WLTP figure for cars registered from April 2020. A fully electric car is always 0g/km.

More info

For plug-in hybrids with CO2 of 50g/km or less, the tax depends on how far the car can travel on electric power alone. It is the “WLTP electric range” (sometimes called EAER) on the manufacturer’s specification or a lease quote.

More info

Diesel cars that do not meet the RDE2 standard pay a 4% supplement. Most diesels registered from September 2018 onwards with a Euro status of 6d meet it; the V5C shows the Euro status.

Where to find it
Where to find itThe P11D value is the car's list price when new, including VAT, delivery and any optional extras, but not the first registration fee or road tax. You will find it on a company car lease quote, the dealer’s or manufacturer’s price list, or last year’s P11D form if the company already has the car. It is the list price, not what the company actually pays, even for a used car.
More info

The rate on the top slice of your income. With a £12,570 salary, dividends above £37,700 are in the higher rate band. If you live in Scotland, the car benefit is taxed at Scottish Income Tax rates, but dividends are taxed at UK rates wherever you live, so the Scottish options pair each Scottish rate with the UK dividend rate it usually sits alongside. For example, a director in the Scottish higher rate band (income from £43,663 to £75,000) usually has dividends above £50,270, taxed at 35.75%.

More info

Journeys for work, not your normal commute. If you ran your own car, the company could pay you 55p a mile tax-free for the first 10,000 business miles and 25p after that.

Tailor it to you: running costs, fuel and Corporation Tax
More info

If the company pays for fuel for private journeys in a petrol, diesel or hybrid car, there is an extra fuel benefit charge of £29,200 × the car's percentage, whatever the fuel actually costs. It rarely pays. Electricity for an electric car is not treated as fuel.

Assumptions (5)
  • HMRC's 2026/27 company car percentages, with CO2 rounded down to the nearest 5g/km; the 4% diesel supplement; the £29,200 fuel benefit multiplier; Class 1A National Insurance at 15%; mileage at 55p for the first 10,000 business miles and 25p after.
  • The comparison measures how much less you end up with each year. Money the company spends is money it cannot pay you as dividends, so it is converted at your dividend tax rate. The monthly cost of the car is assumed to be the same whether the company or you lease it.
  • Leased cars over 50g/km: 15% of the lease payments are not deductible for Corporation Tax. Bought cars: new electric cars get a 100% first-year allowance until 31 March 2027; other cars get 14% a year (50g/km or less) or 6% a year (over 50g/km).
  • Not included: VAT (the company may reclaim some VAT on a lease), and HMRC's temporary easement for some plug-in hybrids tested under the newer Euro 6e-bis standard.
  • Figures are worked to the penny: each amount is rounded to the nearest penny as it is calculated, and every later figure is built from those amounts.
